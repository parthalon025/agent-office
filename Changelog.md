| x1.7 | Fluent and Fluent Dark themes. A fourth theme, `fluent` (Fluent), beside Day, Night and Sublevel: a light theme after the open, MIT-licensed Fluent 2 design tokens and the light layered-surface look. Tokens: a `fluent` value for every color token and `shadow-panel` (a two-layer elevation shadow), and new `light-fluent-*`, `fx-*-fluent` (soft daylight, low bloom, light haze, no vignette), `font-fluent-sans` and `font-fluent-mono` (system faces: Segoe UI Variable and Cascadia Mono with fallbacks; no font file) and `radius-fluent-*` (4, 4 and 8 px). `bundle.css` sets the faces and the radii on `[data-theme|="fluent"]` (Fluent and Fluent Dark), and adds sentence-case panel heads, semibold buttons, an accent pill on the current floor row, the selected bot row and the pressed mode segment, and a darker field underline. Scene: the `fluent` look (`LOOKS`, `FX`), a pale sky with a light skyline in the window and light frames. New `AgentOffice.MODES`: the one list of themes (look id, `data-theme` value, label, icon); the engine (theme to look, look to theme, the `light-*` and `fx-*` reads), `ModeToggle`, the `OfficeScene` HUD toggle and the command palette read it, so each lists Fluent and Fluent Dark. Five themes and Tour fit a narrow box: in a container under 520 px, a `ModeToggle` option with an icon shows the icon only and the pressed option keeps its label (the label is in `.ao-seg-l`, kept for screen readers and as the tooltip). The office top bar keeps to the rows above its side columns: one row above 820 px, two rows to 521 px, three below. In the bar the mode toggle shows icons only (the pressed mode keeps its label) to 999 px, and to 1199 px in Sublevel. The status counts go to a second row between the columns, and hide below 960 px. When a toggle still goes to a lower row (Sublevel from 821 to 899 px, or a long building or agent name), `.ao-hud-left` and `.ao-hud-right` start below the last toggle of the bar (CSS anchor positioning, `anchor-name: --ao-hud-seg`), never above their own top. Before, the sixth segment pushed the view toggle under the Floors panel at 530 to 680 px and 830 to 970 px (to 1170 px in Sublevel), and the status counts touched the right column at 830 to 970 px (on main too, at 830 to 910 px). New icon `fluent` (a round window with a wave). New component `FluentOffice`. Types: `theme` and `mode` take `fluent` and `fluent-dark`; `BotLineup`, `BuildingView`, `RoomScene` and `Workstations` `mode` take `sublevel`, `fluent` and `fluent-dark` (`ModeToggle` options take `icon` since x1.6.8). ContrastCheck and `test/contrast.test.mjs` pass in Fluent; the test now also pins `on-accent` on `accent`, `on-floor` on every `floor-*` (4.9:1), `type-*` on `chip` and `accent` on `bg`, in every theme. The x1.6.8 token checks in `test/ds-kit.test.mjs` read the themes from `tokens.json`, so the per-theme shadow is checked in the Fluent block too, and the ModeToggle check reads the modes from `AO.MODES`. Fluent has no office type thumbnails of its own yet: `OfficeTypeCard` shows the Day set (agent-office #112). Fluent shows no product logo, name or art. Fluent Dark: a fifth theme, `fluent-dark` (Fluent Dark), the same office at night after the dark Fluent 2 alias tokens. Tokens: a `fluent-dark` value for every color token (neutral surfaces `#1f1f1f`, `#292929` glass and `#333333` chips, accent `#4aa0f6` (a step above the Fluent 2 `#479ef5`, so accent text on a chip is 4.5:1) with dark `on-accent`, the light status tints, a dark `on-status`, a black `scrim`, a light `bubble` with dark `status-*-on-bubble`) and `shadow-panel` (the deeper two-layer dark shadow), and new `light-fluent-dark-*` (a dim interior with the lamps on, softer than Night) and `fx-*-fluent-dark` (screens bloom less than Night, a haze, a light vignette). Fluent and Fluent Dark are one family: every Fluent rule in `bundle.css` (faces, radii, heads, buttons, field underline, accent pills) uses `[data-theme|="fluent"]`, which matches both ids, so no rule is copied; the one Fluent Dark rule of its own is the dark color scheme, shared with Night. New field `dark` in `AgentOffice.MODES` (`true` for Night and Fluent Dark): the engine sets `stage.K.dark` from it, and the window light, the monitor glow, the terminal desk light (`public/terminal.js`) and the pinned note card (`public/board.js`) read `K.dark`, not the look name, so Fluent Dark gets the night behaviour. Scene: the `fluent-dark` look (`LOOKS`, `FX`), a dusk sky with a crescent moon over a dark skyline with a few lit windows, and dark frames. New icon `fluentdark` (the Fluent window with a filled night sky and a crescent: the one large fill in the set, so it differs from `fluent` at 14 px, where the toggle shows icons only). `OfficeTypeCard`: a dark theme with no set of its own shows the Night set (Fluent Dark), any other the Day set. `FluentOffice` documents both modes (pass `theme: "fluent-dark"`). Types: `ThemeId` and `Look` take `fluent-dark`, and `ModeDef` has `dark?`. The ColorRatio structure swatch is `floor-ops` unless the theme is Sublevel (carpet), so a new theme needs no entry. The unused `isNight` helper is removed. The check mark of the finished bubble (`StatusBubble` and the 3D bubble sprite) is drawn in `bubble`, a cut-out of the bubble face, not in `on-status`: the dark Fluent Dark `on-status` was 2.77:1 on the disk, and `bubble` is 4.58:1 or more on `status-finished-on-bubble` in every theme (Day and Fluent look the same: there `bubble` and `on-status` are both white). `on-status` is for text on a status fill only (that text is under 4.5:1 in Day, Night, Sublevel and Fluent: agent-office #118). The browser draws scrollbars and check boxes in the scheme of the theme: `[data-theme]` sets `color-scheme: light`, the `dark: true` themes (Night, Fluent Dark) set `dark`, and OS dark mode with no theme sets `dark`; it replaces the dark rule for two inbox check boxes, and the notes editor (a light face) keeps `light`. Before, Night and Fluent Dark showed white scrollbar tracks on dark panels. `test/contrast.test.mjs` and ContrastCheck also pin `bubble` on `status-finished-on-bubble` (3:1) and `accent` on `chip` (4.5:1, accent text on a selected row; Day is 4.21:1, an older gap held as a known failure, agent-office #117). ContrastCheck and `test/contrast.test.mjs` pass in Fluent Dark; the smallest margin is `accent` on `chip` (4.60:1). |
| x1.6.8 | Honest headers (agent-office #87). The `tokens.css` header and the asset block header in `bundle.js` now say the file is edited by hand; no generator is in the repo. The DS-README quickstart row for `tokens.css` and the Reference label say the same. `test/ds-kit.test.mjs` checks every non-color token group of `tokens.json` (type, spacing, radius, shadow, blur, opacity, zIndex, duration, easing, breakpoint, scene, light, fx) against `tokens.css`, with the same value, and the per-theme shadow in each theme block. `bundle.js` has one `ModeToggle` (the first copy, with no icons and no Sublevel, is removed; the live one is unchanged). `icon` is in `ModeToggleProps.options`, and the docs list Day, Night, Sublevel and Tour. | x1.6.7 | GPU memory. `Stage.dispose` frees every geometry, material and texture of the scene, the Kit caches, the light shadows and the post effects (composer passes and render targets), then the renderer and its WebGL context (`forceContextLoss`). Before, it freed only the renderer, so each scene remount (City to building, a rebuild) left its whole GPU scene behind. It is safe to call twice, and it frees nothing that another live stage still uses. `stage.clear(group)` frees materials and textures too, not only geometries, after the next frame, so what the scene and the Kit caches still use then stays. `Kit.refresh` frees the textures it drops on a theme change (#84). |
| x1.6.6 | Subagents. A temporary subagent reaches its desk before it leaves: `retire` waits until the bot has stopped walking, lets it sit 1.4 s (`DUR.dwell`), then sends it to the elevator. A bot that stops moving for 15 s, or any bot after 120 s, is removed; a long walk in or out is never cut short, so a far room never makes the bot vanish before the elevator. A bot whose floor goes off screen during the walk out is removed at once. The same rule covers session blobs (`session.end`), which use `retire` too. Before, a fixed 1.4 s timer sent a bot back out while it still walked in from the elevator. |
| x1.6.5 | Checker and tokens. New token `scrim` (`#0a1016` in every theme), the dark veil behind a dialog or the command palette; `bundle.css` uses it for `.ao-overlay` (45%) and `.ao-overlay.is-inline` (25%) in place of two `rgba()` literals, and the Sublevel scan lines on `.ao-fh` mix `on-floor` in place of `rgba(255,255,255,.06)`; the look is the same (#59). `kit/check.py` checks a name built at run time (`"var(--status-" + s`, `"role-" + r`) by its prefix: some token or icon must start with it. The old `ModeToggle` in `bundle.js` (replaced by the icon version at load) loses its text glyphs. CI now runs `kit/check.py` on `components/bundle.css`, `components/bundle.js` and `kit/page.html`. |

When the browser storage is full, the saved history of the building seen longest ago goes first, then the oldest half of this one. |
