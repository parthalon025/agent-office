# SolutionOffice

The Microsoft customer solutions office: the roles that take a customer from first call to value, as agents.

| Floor | Room (type) | Bots (role) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 Frontier | Frontier War Room (war room) | Forward Deployed Engineer, Account Planner (AE), Exec Sponsor Brief, Value Tracker |
| 4 Customer Success | Success Desk (team) | CSAM · Contoso, CSAM · Fabrikam, Adoption Coach (CSAM), Support Escalation |
| 3 Architecture | CSA Bench (team), Landing Zone Lab (lab) | CSA · AI Apps, CSA · Data & AI, CSA · Infrastructure; Deployment Bot (FDE), Well-Architected Check |
| 2 Solution Engineering | Demo Lab (lab), Envisioning Studio (studio) | SE · AI Business, SE · Cloud & AI, SE · Security; Envisioning Lead (SE), Demo Producer |
| 1 Account Team | Account Team (team) | Account Executive, Solution Specialist, Partner Manager |

The customers are Microsoft's fictional sample companies (Contoso, Fabrikam). The office uses no Microsoft logos. Replace the bots with your own agents through an adapter.
